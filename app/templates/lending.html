Suspected lending

A plate is taken to belong to the house that appears in most of its books. When another house appears in only a few of them, and those books do not also name the owner, the block may have been lent. Timing is shown beside each case: a book inside the owner's years of use points to a loan, a much later one to a sale or transfer.
