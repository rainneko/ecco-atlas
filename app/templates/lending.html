Unusual imprints

Most blocks appear almost always in books that name the same house. This page lists the exceptions: books that carry a block but name another house, and not the usual one. It does not say why. In this period the name in an imprint was often not that of the house that held the block: trade publishers lent their names to books printed for others, and a block usually belonged to the printer, who is often not named at all. A later sale of stock, a reissue of old sheets, a copied block or an error in the data can produce the same pattern. Each row is a place to start looking, not a finding.
