Step 2 of 4 — what type of ornament is this?

Each score is the mean similarity to the ten most similar of 300 sample ornaments of that type. Pick another type if the guess looks wrong, then continue.

Matching usually takes about 1 s.

Your image is kept on the server for 24 hours so that the result page can be revisited or shared; it is not added to the atlas. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
